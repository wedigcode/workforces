# Strategic Advisory & Problem Discovery (/wf-advisor)

Universal consultative problem discovery, strategic trade-off evaluation, and architecture guidance. Led by `@advisor`.

---

## 1. The 5-Dimension JTBD Discovery Protocol

Engage in a 1-to-2 question-per-turn dialogue exploring the 5 dimensions of customer pain:

1. **Root Problem & Situational Catalyst**: What is fundamentally broken? What specific event triggered the user to seek a solution today?
2. **3D Pain Points & Friction**: What hurts most across **Functional tasks**, **Emotional anxieties**, and **Social perceptions**?
3. **Persona & Raw Voice**: Who suffers daily, and how do they describe the pain in their exact words?
4. **Failed Workarounds**: How are teams currently coping (spreadsheets, manual glue), and why do existing tools fail?
5. **Value Breakthrough & Stakes**: How does a 10x solution expand **Willingness to Pay (WTP)**? What is the cost of inaction if unsolved for 6 months?

---

## 2. The 4-Step Executive Decision Sequence

When evaluating feature proposals or strategic pivots, audit against this sequence:

| Step | Focus Area | Hurdle / Verification Rule |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **JTBD & Customer Validation** | Reject solutions lacking a causal situational trigger. |
| **2** | **Value Stick Audit** | Must lengthen total stick (drive WTP up or supplier WTS down); no zero-sum margin extraction. |
| **3** | **Growth Loops & Dynamics** | Identify closed compounding loop (viral, UGC, paid reinvestment, or marketplace). |
| **4** | **Unit Economics & Execution** | Verify $\text{LTV:CAC} \ge 3\times$ and Payback $< 12\text{mo}$. Apply Sense-Seize-Transform. |

---

## 3. Bottleneck Diagnosis (Theory of Constraints)

Subordinate all secondary optimizations to solving the single primary throughput constraint:
- **Market Awareness**: Users don't know the solution exists $\rightarrow$ Focus on top-of-funnel outbound/SEO.
- **Activation Friction**: Users drop off before experiencing "aha" $\rightarrow$ Strip onboarding friction.
- **Core Value Realization**: Product fails primary JTBD $\rightarrow$ Fix core utility before adding features.
- **Retention / Churn**: Initial value fades $\rightarrow$ Deepen compounding workflow utility.
- **Unit Economics**: Payback > 12mo or inverted LTV:CAC $\rightarrow$ Restructure pricing and acquisition model.
