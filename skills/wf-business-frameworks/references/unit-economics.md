# SaaS & Customer Unit Economics

Top-line revenue growth without healthy underlying unit economics leads to unsustainable burn. Modern MBA curricula and venture benchmarks evaluate scalable software and business models through strict unit-level formulas and hurdles.

---

## 📐 Core Formulas

### 1. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

$$\text{LTV} = \frac{\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin \%}}{\text{Churn Rate}}$$

- **$\text{ARPU}$**: Average Revenue Per User / Account per month.
- **$\text{Gross Margin \%}$**: $\frac{\text{Revenue} - \text{COGS}}{\text{Revenue}}$ (hosting, customer support, third-party API costs).
- **$\text{Churn Rate}$**: Monthly customer cancellation or logo churn percentage.

---

### 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

$$\text{CAC} = \frac{\text{Total Sales \& Marketing Expenses in Period}}{\text{Total New Customers Acquired in Period}}$$

- Includes all ad spend, marketing salaries, sales commissions, tooling, and onboarding software.

---

### 3. LTV : CAC Ratio

$$\text{LTV : CAC} = \frac{\text{Customer Lifetime Value}}{\text{Customer Acquisition Cost}}$$

- **Benchmark**: $\ge 3.0\times$ (Healthy, scalable business).
- **$< 1.0\times$**: Value destroying (losing money on every acquired customer).
- **$> 5.0\times$**: Under-investing in growth (opportunity to aggressively scale acquisition).

---

### 4. CAC Payback Period (Months)

$$\text{CAC Payback Period} = \frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin \%}}$$

- **Elite Venture Benchmark**: $< 12\text{ months}$ (capital is returned within 1 year).
- **Consumer SaaS / Self-Serve Benchmark**: $< 6\text{ months}$.
- **Enterprise High-ACV Benchmark**: $12\text{--}18\text{ months}$ (acceptable only with multi-year upfront contracts).

---

## 🎯 Executive Health Summary Table

| Metric | Danger Zone | Good Benchmark | Elite / High Scale |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **LTV : CAC** | $< 2.0\times$ | $3.0\times \text{--} 4.0\times$ | $\ge 4.5\times$ |
| **CAC Payback** | $> 18\text{ mo}$ | $8\text{--}12\text{ mo}$ | $< 6\text{ mo}$ |
| **Gross Margin** | $< 60\%$ | $70\%\text{--}80\%$ | $\ge 80\%$ |
| **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** | $< 95\%$ | $105\%\text{--}115\%$ | $\ge 125\%$ |
