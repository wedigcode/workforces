# Value-Based Strategy & The Value Stick Framework

The **Value Stick** decomposes enterprise value creation into a vertical continuum bounded by two fundamental economic thresholds:
- **Willingness to Pay (WTP)**: The absolute maximum price a customer is willing to pay for a product or service.
- **Willingness to Sell (WTS)**: The absolute minimum compensation suppliers or workers are willing to accept to supply their goods or labor.

Between these outer boundaries sit the realized market variables of **Price** and **Cost**.

---

## 📊 The Four Thresholds and Three Wedges

```
[ Upper Boundary ]  --- Willingness to Pay (WTP) ---
                                ▲
                                │  Customer Delight Wedge  = (WTP - Price)
                                ▼
                    --- Price ----------------------
                                ▲
                                │  Firm Margin Wedge       = (Price - Cost)
                                ▼
                    --- Cost -----------------------
                                ▲
                                │  Supplier Surplus Wedge  = (Cost - WTS)
                                ▼
[ Lower Boundary ]  --- Willingness to Sell (WTS) --
```

### The Three Value Wedges:
1. **Customer Delight ($\text{WTP} - \text{Price}$)**: The consumer surplus. The extra perceived value received above what was paid. High delight drives retention, brand moat, and organic advocacy.
2. **Firm Margin ($\text{Price} - \text{Cost}$)**: The net economic profit captured by the business.
3. **Supplier Surplus ($\text{Cost} - \text{WTS}$)**: The surplus captured by workers, vendors, and partners. When suppliers are well-compensated or productivity tools lower their friction, they provide higher quality and loyalty.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Implication: "Lengthen the Total Stick"

In traditional zero-sum thinking, firms attempt to expand their margin by:
- Raising **Price** (which shrinks Customer Delight $\to$ invites competitors)
- Squeezing **Cost** paid to suppliers (which shrinks Supplier Surplus $\to$ degrades quality)

### The Value-Based Strategic Imperative:
Enduring competitive advantage comes from **lengthening the stick**:

1. **Drive WTP Up (Value Creation for Customers)**:
   - Deepen product quality, remove user cognitive friction, add intelligent automation, build trust and brand resonance.
   - Allows the firm to raise prices without shrinking Customer Delight.

2. **Drive WTS Down (Value Creation for Suppliers & Workers)**:
   - Provide software tooling, automated workflows, reliable demand, or improved working conditions that make suppliers happier to work with the firm even at lower unit costs.
   - Example: Providing delivery drivers with route optimization software that increases their effective hourly yield.

---

## 🛠️ Operational Audit Questions

When evaluating any product feature, pricing change, or vendor decision:
- [ ] Does this proposal raise customer **WTP** or lower partner **WTS**?
- [ ] Are we expanding the total value stick, or are we simply engaging in a zero-sum price/margin squeeze?
- [ ] How does this change affect the **Customer Delight wedge**? Will it trigger customer churn or competitive vulnerability?
