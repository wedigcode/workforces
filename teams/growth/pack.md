# Team Pack Building Block: Growth & SEO

## Domain Purpose
Optimizes search engine visibility (SEO), AI Search Engine Optimization (GEO/AISO), domain site buildouts, and customer acquisition loops.

---

## Principles of Domain Excellence

1. **Intent Matching & Authority:**
   - Align content directly with user search intent (Informational, Commercial, Navigational, Transactional).
   - Build domain authority via structured data, fast Core Web Vitals, and deep contextual relevance.

2. **AI Search Engine Optimization (AISO/GEO):**
   - Structure content so AI assistants (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Claude, Gemini) easily extract direct answers and cite sources.

3. **Growth Focus by Business Model:**
   - **SaaS:** Focus on product-led SEO, comparison pages (Vs / Alternatives), and programmatic landing pages.
   - **Local Service:** Focus on Google Business Profile, local keyword targeting (City + Service), and review generation loops.
   - **Enterprise:** Focus on whitepapers, industry report indexing, and executive thought leadership.

---

## Reusable Heuristics & Building Blocks

- **SEO Fundamentals:** Canonical tags, sitemaps, OpenGraph metadata, schema markup.
- **Site Foundation:** Semantic HTML, clean routing, mobile optimization.

---

## SOP / Workflow Patterns
When generating a growth team for a project, consider whether the project needs:
- `seo-audit` (Technical and content SEO audit)
- `domain-setup` (Domain foundation, site architecture, and AI protocol files)
- `intent-arbitrageur` (Identifying high-converting, low-competition search queries)
