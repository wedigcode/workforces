# Team Pack Building Block: Growth & SEO

## Domain Purpose
Optimizes search engine visibility (SEO), AI Search Engine Optimization (GEO/AISO), programmatic page generation, intent matching, and customer acquisition/conversion loops.

---

## Principles of Domain Excellence

1. **Intent Matching & Authority:**
   - Align content directly with explicit user search intent (Informational, Commercial, Navigational, Transactional).
   - Build domain authority via structured schema markup, fast Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS), and deep contextual relevance.

2. **Technical & Programmatic SEO:**
   - Build scalable, programmatic landing page frameworks backed by clean site architecture, automated sitemaps, canonical tags, and dynamic metadata routing.
   - Maintain flawless crawlability, indexation efficiency, dynamic rendering, and internal link graph health.

3. **AI Search Engine Optimization (AISO / GEO):**
   - Optimize content for generative engine discovery across platforms like Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Claude, and Gemini.
   - Author clear, structured, fact-dense direct answers, tables, key takeaway summaries, and citable semantic references that AI search models index and cite prominently.

4. **Conversion Loop Optimization:**
   - Design continuous experimentation loops (A/B testing, headline tuning, micro-interaction CTAs, friction reduction) to maximize lead conversion and user retention.
   - Track acquisition-to-activation user pathways, optimizing page speed and intent congruence at every funnel node.

5. **Growth Focus by Business Model:**
   - **SaaS:** Focus on product-led SEO, programmatic comparison pages (Vs / Alternatives), feature landers, and free tool acquisition funnels.
   - **Local Service:** Focus on Google Business Profile, localized keyword clusters (City + Service), schema location data, and review generation loops.
   - **Enterprise:** Focus on whitepapers, industry report indexing, executive thought leadership, and gated research hubs.

---

## Team Roles & Personas

- **Technical & Programmatic SEO Lead:** Oversees site crawlability, schema taxonomy, Core Web Vitals optimization, indexing rules, and scalable programmatic template architectures.
- **AISO / AI Search Optimization Specialist:** Engineers content structure and entity relationships to maximize citation rates across Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Gemini, and generative AI search engines.
- **Intent & Content Growth Strategist:** Analyzes user search intent, maps keyword clusters to conversion funnels, executes intent-arbitrage opportunities, and drives high-ROI content roadmaps.

---

## SOP / Workflow Patterns
When generating a growth team for a project, consider whether the project needs:
- `seo-technical-audit` (Comprehensive crawl, indexability, schema, and Core Web Vitals audit)
- `aiso-optimization` (Structuring content, entities, direct answers, and semantic citations for generative search engines)
- `intent-arbitrage` (Identifying high-converting, low-competition search queries and intent gaps)
- `conversion-loop-optimization` (Funnel analysis, micro-copy tweaks, and acquisition CTA testing)
