# Team Pack Building Block: Marketing & Brand Strategy

## Domain Purpose
Drives brand awareness, positioning, client journey mapping, content creation, and conversion copy across customer touchpoints.

---

## Principles of Domain Excellence

1. **Customer-Centric Positioning:**
   - Great marketing speaks to customer pain points and desired outcomes, not feature lists.
   - Match messaging to the customer's awareness stage (Problem-Aware -> Solution-Aware -> Product-Aware).

2. **Visual & Copy Consistency:**
   - Maintain uniform voice, typography, whitespace, and visual contrast.
   - Avoid empty corporate buzzwords; prioritize clear, punchy, high-converting language.

3. **Journey Alignment by Business Model:**
   - **SaaS:** Focus on self-serve onboarding, product-led trial hooks, and instant value metrics.
   - **Local Service:** Focus on local trust/reputation, service radius clarity, clear contact/phone CTA, and appointment booking integration.
   - **Enterprise:** Focus on ROI case studies, security/compliance posture, executive summaries, and whitepaper lead magnets.

---

## Reusable Heuristics & Building Blocks

- **Copywriting:** Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS) structure, Attention-Interest-Desire-Action (AIDA).
- **Design:** Modern dark/light UI modes, clear visual hierarchy, accessible contrast ratios.
- **Content Strategy:** Topic clusters, intent matching, channel-specific formatting (LinkedIn vs Email vs Landing Page).

---

## SOP / Workflow Patterns
When generating a marketing team for a project, consider whether the project needs:
- `launch-campaign` (Campaign planning and cross-channel asset execution)
- `landing-page` (High-converting landing page structure and copy)
- `lead-magnet` (Valuable opt-in resource definition)
