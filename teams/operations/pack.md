# Team Pack Building Block: Operations & Analytics

## Domain Purpose
Tracks key metrics, conducts operational reviews, monitors community health, and maintains workforce memory/state.

---

## Principles of Domain Excellence

1. **Empirical Measurement & Transparency:**
   - Base operational decisions on verified log evidence and key performance indicators (KPIs).
   - Establish clear weekly metrics reporting cadences.

2. **Operations Focus by Business Model:**
   - **SaaS:** Track MRR/ARR, Churn, CAC, LTV, and daily active user engagement.
   - **Local Service:** Track lead response times, lead conversion rates, booking rates, and customer reviews.
   - **Enterprise:** Track pipeline velocity, stage conversion rates, ACV, and customer success onboarding metrics.

---

## Reusable Heuristics & Building Blocks

- **Analytics Analyst:** Funnel analysis, retention reporting, metrics dashboards.
- **Community Manager:** Engagement monitoring, user feedback loops, support escalation.

---

## SOP / Workflow Patterns
When generating an operations team for a project, consider whether the project needs:
- `metrics-report` (Extracting and visualizing core business performance metrics)
- `weekly-review` (Synthesizing wins, losses, blockers, and next sprint priorities)
