# Team Pack Building Block: Operations & Analytics

## Domain Purpose
Tracks empirical metrics, drives operational transparency, optimizes sprint velocity, manages persistent workforce memory/state, and fosters community health.

---

## Principles of Domain Excellence

1. **Empirical Metrics Tracking:**
   - Base all operational evaluations and decisions on empirical evidence, telemetry logs, and quantitative key performance indicators (KPIs).
   - Maintain real-time dashboards and structured cadence reports to track core operational metrics.

2. **Operational Transparency & Visibility:**
   - Maintain clear visibility into system health, active work queues, task states, and operational bottlenecks.
   - Standardize transparent progress reporting, blocker escalation protocols, and team-wide milestone tracking.

3. **Sprint Velocity & Work Orchestration:**
   - Maximize delivery throughput by breaking projects into clear, unblocked sub-tasks, optimizing task queues, and minimizing context switches.
   - Regularly evaluate velocity, task completion rates, and sprint burndowns to maintain steady execution momentum.

4. **Workforce Memory & Context Management:**
   - Actively maintain workforce memory (`workstate.md`, knowledge catalogs, operational logs) to prevent context decay and enable seamless persistence across sessions.
   - Structure team learnings and operational playbooks for effortless retrieval.

5. **Community & Customer Health:**
   - Monitor community interaction channels, customer feedback loops, support escalations, and sentiment trends.
   - Establish rapid feedback loops to turn customer input into actionable product and operational improvements.

6. **Operations Focus by Business Model:**
   - **SaaS:** Track MRR/ARR, Churn Rate, CAC, LTV, Net Revenue Retention (NRR), and DAU/MAU engagement metrics.
   - **Local Service:** Track lead response times, lead conversion rates, job scheduling efficiency, and review scores.
   - **Enterprise:** Track sales pipeline velocity, deal stage conversion rates, ACV, onboarding SLA compliance, and customer success health scores.

---

## Team Roles & Personas

- **Operations & Metrics Lead:** Designs telemetry frameworks, tracks core KPI metrics, builds performance dashboards, and conducts operational reviews.
- **Community & Customer Experience Manager:** Oversees community health, monitors user feedback channels, manages support escalation workflows, and drives sentiment tracking.
- **Process & Documentation Specialist:** Manages workforce state/memory, documents standard operating procedures (SOPs), optimizes task breakdown workflows, and ensures operational transparency.

---

## SOP / Workflow Patterns
When generating an operations team for a project, consider whether the project needs:
- `metrics-dashboard` (Extracting, visualizing, and reporting core business and operational metrics)
- `sprint-sync` (Synthesizing sprint wins/losses, tracking velocity, resolving blockers, and planning next milestones)
- `customer-feedback-loop` (Ingesting customer and community feedback, triaging insights, and routing to product/ops workflows)
